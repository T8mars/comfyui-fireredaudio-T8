# Resume batch and production state

Restores an AudioBatch from a manifest and shows the production state needed to continue.

## How to use

1. Choose a batch, duration-fit, review, repair, or candidate manifest.
2. Inspect playable, approved, pending, retry, and missing counts.
3. Follow the dashboard action into review, repair, or batch save.

## Important

Export readiness requires every item to exist and be explicitly approved or adopted. Missing files are never hidden.
