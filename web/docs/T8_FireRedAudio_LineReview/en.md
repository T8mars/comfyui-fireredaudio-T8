# Line production review

Combines automatic QA evidence and human listening into approve, review, and retry paths.

## How to use

1. Listen per line and record a decision, 1–5 rating, and note.
2. Connect `retry_line_ids` to corrective retry or the creative candidate pool.
3. Send only `approved_batch` to final delivery.

## Important

`auto` follows the QA suggestion. Critical delivery lines still need human confirmation.
